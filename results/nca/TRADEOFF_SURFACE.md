N=1000 genomes/cell, T_max=40, init_condition=`center_seed` (P3), hidden_channels=3, seed=0. 3D grid: init_gain in [0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.4, 0.7, 1.0] x decay in [0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.5] x state_bound in [1, 2, 5] (6x4x3 = 72 cells).

**Why this grid, not N4's**: N4 (results/nca/TIMESCALE_CALIBRATION.md) swept (decay, state_bound) alone at init_gain=1.0 (N2's value, chosen for near-unit pre-activation variance -- a signal-propagation criterion, never checked against the certificate condition state_bound*K(theta)<1). K(theta) = sqrt(5)*sigma_max(W1)*sigma_max(W2) is controlled by init_gain, not by decay/state_bound (they rescale/shift the update, they do not change the network's own operator norm) -- so N4's sweep never actually varied the quantity the certificate depends on. This is the axis N4 should have swept.

## Notes

`unique_valid_body_fraction` here is unique-body-hash fraction among ALL sampled bodies (matching this module's own single combined measurement loop), not restricted to valid bodies only -- unlike evodevo.analysis.structural.diversity's field of the same name, which restricts to valid bodies. Both are legitimate diversity measures; this module's column name is kept for readability but the denominator differs from structural.diversity's -- do not directly cross-cite the two without this caveat.

This module never computes or consults a fitness, reward, or task-performance quantity: every number above is derived from decode()/develop()/validate_body alone.
