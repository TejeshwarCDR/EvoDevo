| 2026-09-02 | Minimum occupied-voxel count (`min_voxels`) | Accept anything EvoGym itself accepts (as low as 1 connected actuator voxel) vs a stricter project-level floor | `min_voxels=3`, enforced in `evodevo/morphology/validate.py::validate_body` | EvoGym's own `is_connected`/`has_actuator` helpers accept a single-voxel body as a valid robot, but a 1-2 voxel body cannot meaningfully "walk" and would pollute both arms' search spaces with degenerate morphologies that only exist because the encoding happened to produce a near-empty grid. | 2026-09-02 | Direct-encoding real-PPO baseline budget for the roadmap step-6 gate | Full config defaults (1M timesteps/eval, 1000-eval budget: ~22 days/seed, not runnable here) vs a scaled-down but real-PPO budget | `ppo_timesteps=5000`, `population_size=15`, `offspring_size=15`, `eval_budget=300` per seed (5 seeds, Walker-v0) | Measured cost: ~2s fixed overhead + ~1.35ms/timestep per PPO evaluation (20,000 timesteps &asymp; 29s). At the full default budget (1M timesteps &times; 1000 evals) this is ~22 days/seed, infeasible in this environment. A first attempt at 20 evals/seed (pop=5) showed only 1/5 seeds finding any valid body at all (see next row: random direct genomes are valid only ~2-3% of the time under default `empty_prob=0.6`, so a population of 5 has a high chance of starting -- and staying, given `mutation_rate=0.1` -- entirely invalid). Scaling to 300 evals/seed (pop=15) was chosen to give mutation a ~99%+ analytic chance of finding at least one valid body per seed while keeping wall-clock in the tens-of-minutes range. This is a pipeline-validation-scale budget, not the scientific comparison budget; the full 1e6-timestep budget recommended by EvoGym remains a threat-to-validity to disclose in the final report. |
| 2026-09-02 | Direct-encoding random-genome validity rate under default hyperparameters | Accept as-is vs re-tune `empty_prob`/`mutation_rate` now | Left as-is (`empty_prob=0.6`, `mutation_rate=0.1`, matching EvoGym's own GA example convention); NOT re-tuned in this step | Empirically measured: purely random direct genomes are valid (connected, has actuator, &ge;3 voxels) only ~2.25-2.4% of the time (measured both ad hoc, n=5000, and formally via `evodevo/experiments/nca_sanity.py`, n=2000, seed=0: direct validity rate 0.0225). This is a real, reportable property of the direct encoding's prior over morphologies, not a bug. Per the roadmap's fairness protocol (Part 4), per-arm mutation/sampling hyperparameters must only be tuned via a separate, smaller pilot budget and the tuning procedure reported -- not adjusted ad hoc while chasing a passing baseline run. That pilot-tuning step is deferred to the fairness-harness prompt; for now the EA's `eval_budget` was scaled up instead (see row above) to work around the low validity rate without touching encoder hyperparameters. |
| 2026-09-02 | E0 sanity gate (roadmap step-8 hard gate): NCA validity rate and degeneracy at default hyperparameters | STOP and revisit `init_scale`/initial state/`hidden_channels` if validity &lt;10% or degenerate-body fraction &gt;50%, vs proceed as-designed if both pass | PASS as-designed; no changes made to `evodevo/encoders/nca.py`'s default hyperparameters (`init_scale=0.5`, `hidden_channels=3`, `dev_steps=10`) | `evodevo/experiments/nca_sanity.py`, N=2000, seed=0: NCA validity rate = 0.6915 (>> 10% gate) and degenerate-body fraction (all-empty + uniform-material) = 0.0195 (<< 50% gate). Both comfortably pass; no revision needed. As a side reference from the same run, the direct encoder's random-genome validity rate was 0.0225 -- i.e. at these defaults the NCA's prior over morphologies is dramatically more likely to produce a valid, differentiated body than the direct encoding's, a genuinely interesting pre-registered finding about the two encodings' priors (see `results/nca_sanity.md`). One real limitation surfaced by the same run and recorded here per the roadmap's instruction to report it regardless of gate outcome: 68.35% of genomes were still changing on the very last developmental step (t=9&rarr;t=10) and never observably settled within `dev_steps=10`; mean max-abs residual-state magnitude at t=T was ~40.7 (vs. an initial-state scale of 0.1), indicating the residual update is not bounded/self-stabilizing at these defaults. This developmental-instability limitation does not block the comparison but belongs in the final report's threats-to-validity section. |
